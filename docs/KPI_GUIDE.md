# Portfolio KPI Guide

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md)

The finance case studies use scenario datasets to demonstrate program control, FP&A variance analysis, executive reporting, peer benchmarking, and long-range planning.

## Program Finance Control Tower

**Business purpose:** identify workstreams that need forecast review before a monthly business review.

**Key indicators:**
- Portfolio EAC Variance
- Weighted CPI
- Projected Margin at Completion
- Watchlist Workstream Rate
- Portfolio Cost Variance
- Median Period Burn Rate

## Budget vs. Actual FP&A Variance Analysis

**Business purpose:** explain monthly spending performance and identify variance drivers.

**Key indicators:**
- Net Budget Variance
- Forecast Accuracy
- Unfavorable Variance Exposure
- Material Variance Rate
- Average Monthly Absolute Variance
- Largest Monthly Variance

## Defense Contractor Peer Benchmarking

**Business purpose:** size a benchmark market, assess concentration, and establish external growth references for planning scenarios.

**Key indicators:**
- Latest-Year Benchmark Revenue
- Top 5 Revenue Concentration
- USA Revenue Share
- Eligible Median Peer CAGR
- Eligible Top-Quartile CAGR
- 3-Year Base LRP Growth

## Status convention

Where scorecards use Green, Yellow, and Red status thresholds:

- **Green** — within the project’s planning tolerance
- **Yellow** — review recommended
- **Red** — management attention required

The thresholds are analytical conventions for the portfolio scenarios, not universal accounting standards.
